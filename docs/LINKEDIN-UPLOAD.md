# LinkedIn Project Document — How to Upload

A polished **5-page project showcase** is ready at:

**`docs/linkedin-project-showcase.html`**

## Export to PDF (recommended)

1. Open `docs/linkedin-project-showcase.html` in **Chrome** or **Edge**
2. Press `Cmd + P` (Mac) or `Ctrl + P` (Windows)
3. Destination: **Save as PDF**
4. Paper size: **A4**
5. Margins: **Default** or **None**
6. Enable **Background graphics** (so colors print correctly)
7. Save as `Mutual-Fund-RAG-FAQ-Assistant.pdf`

## Upload to LinkedIn

1. Start a new post on LinkedIn
2. Click **Add a document** (paperclip / document icon)
3. Upload the PDF
4. Add your caption (see suggested post below)
5. Post
